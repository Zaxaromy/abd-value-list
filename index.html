Game info

Everything a new player needs to get started.

What is Anime Ball?

A fast, competitive ball game where anime-inspired characters use special abilities to out-rally opponents.

Controls

Move, hit and use your ability with the on-screen or keyboard bindings. Time your hit as the ball nears you for the strongest return.

Getting characters

Unlock characters through spins, events and trading with other players.

Traits

Traits are rolled bonuses that change how a character plays. See Best Traits for the top picks.

Trading safely

Only trade in the game’s official trade menu. Never share your account details.

Tips for beginners

Learn one character first, practise your timing, and check values before accepting a trade.
